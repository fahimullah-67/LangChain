Under the vast green field where legends rise,
Beneath the roar of countless cries,
Stands a team in emerald pride,
With dreams of glory burning inside.

From dusty streets to stadium lights,
They carry hopes through days and nights,
Each swing of bat, each flying ball,
Echoes a nation standing tall.

From Lahore to Karachi, the passion flows,
In every heart, the spirit grows,
Children dream with eyes so bright,
To wear that jersey, green and white.

Fast bowlers roar like thunder in rain,
Delivering fire again and again,
With pace so fierce, the stumps take flight,
A moment of magic, a thrilling sight.

Batsmen stand with courage and grace,
Facing each ball, they hold their place,
A cover drive cuts through the air,
A perfect stroke beyond compare.

Through victories that make hearts sing,
And losses that pain they sometimes bring,
They rise again with stronger will,
Determined to conquer every hill.

Crowds erupt with chants so loud,
Each player feels the nation proud,
Flags are waving, voices unite,
Turning the darkness into light.

Not just a game, but a story deep,
Of promises made and dreams to keep,
Of heroes born in moments rare,
With fearless hearts and endless care.

From World Cup nights to rival fights,
They shine beneath the brightest lights,
Writing history, page by page,
Leaving their mark on cricket’s stage.

So here they stand, through thick and thin,
With burning hearts and strength within,
A symbol of hope, a nation’s flame,
Pakistan lives in every game.
